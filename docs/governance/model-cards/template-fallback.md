# Model card: deterministic templates (non-AI baseline)

This component has no model. It is the baseline every AI path falls back to, and the only text the automation may send without a draft. Template: [README.md](README.md).

## Summary

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Model / version id | None. `template_set_version: 3` in `config/outreach_policy.yaml`; the template provider is `TemplateProvider` (name `template`) |
| Kind | Deterministic text templates filled with three fields |

## Intended use

One reviewed text per purpose (result notification, reminder, appointment confirmation, refusal acknowledgement and others in `PURPOSES`), channel (SMS, WhatsApp, letter) and language (English, Arabic), filled only with the care owner's name, the next step and, for a confirmation, the appointment date. It is (1) the default draft when `LLM_PROVIDER=template`, (2) the fallback whenever a model fails or is rejected, and (3) the only message the automation actor `system:automation` may send without per-message approval, and only for templates listed at their version in a signed outreach policy.

## Out-of-scope uses

Anything outside the matrix, adapting to the patient, answering questions, stating a result or any clinical content. Automatic sending with an unsigned policy: `approved_by` is `null`, so the append-time guard refuses every automatic `message_sent` (the demo's `OUTREACH_DEMO_APPROVAL=1` shows a visible placeholder and is refused outside `APP_ENV=dev`).

## Data

No data and no training. The texts are hand-written by the developers; the Arabic was revised after AI pre-reviews of 2026-10-18 ([arabic-review.md](../arabic-review.md), [clinician-review.md](../clinician-review.md)) and has had no native-speaker or clinician review (H1, H2). Reviewer packs are generated by `tools/review/build_packs.py`.

## Evaluation

- Validator benign controls RT-V-15: 38 of 38 pass (every purpose, channel and language template, plus the two sample messages must be accepted by the validator); source `tests/redteam/results.json`, cases `RT-V-15.*`, definition in [red-team.md](../red-team.md) section 5.1.
- Readability: `test_english_reads_at_plain_level` in `api/tests/test_ai_templates.py` asserts no English sentence over 22 words and a mean of at most 12 words per sentence, a rough proxy for grade 6. There is no validated Arabic readability measure.
- Other tests in the same file: the full matrix is present, every text renders and fits its channel limit even with worst-case field lengths, no forbidden style words, the Arabic is Arabic script and marked for review, the result notification names no finding and says who will call.

## Limitations

- Fixed wording cannot cover every situation; it is deliberately plain and may read as impersonal.
- Reading level for Arabic is not measured; the English test is a proxy, not a validated readability score.
- Arabic and English wording unsigned by a clinician (H1) and by a native speaker (H2); editing a template and bumping its version makes the policy fail to load until re-approved.
- Gulf-region vocabulary and other languages are not covered; other languages are drafted in English.

## Subgroup performance

Only English and Arabic exist. On the validator, 0 of 148 benign messages were rejected in each language (every template, four owner names, with and without a date, plus the at-limit texts; [fairness.md](../../results/fairness.md)). No evidence about how patients of any group understand the texts exists, and the Arabic has had no native-speaker review (H2).

## Human oversight

The clinical lead must sign `config/outreach_policy.yaml` (H1) before automatic messaging runs on real data; until then nothing is sent automatically. The weekly cap (`max_auto_messages_per_week: 2`), consent, and opt-out or wrong-number holds are enforced at append time. Approved drafts that fell back to a template still need a person's approval.

## Monitoring

Exists in code today: `/health` reports whether the outreach policy is usable and signed; the automation logs `outreach_held {reason: policy_unsigned}` once per case; tests render and validate every template in CI. Abstention counts (`GET /metrics/abstention`) show how often the fallback is used. No alert on a template defect exists; a template that fails to load stops automatic messaging loudly. Incidents: [ai-incidents.md](../ai-incidents.md).

## Kill switch

This is the safe state, not a risk to switch off. To stop automatic template sending: leave `approved_by: null` in `config/outreach_policy.yaml` and set `OUTREACH_DEMO_APPROVAL=0`, or set `AUTOMATION_ENABLED=false` (`api/app/automation.py:498`, `api/app/routers/scheduler.py:179`); see [ai-incidents.md](../ai-incidents.md) section 4.
