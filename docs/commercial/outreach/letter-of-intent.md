**From:** [Provider legal name], [address] ("the Provider"). **To:** [Operator name] ("the Operator"). **Date:** [date].

1. **Purpose.** The parties intend to run a read-only shadow pilot to test whether giving every positive screening result a named owner, a clock and an audit trail finds cases that nobody is tracking. This letter records intent. It is not a contract.

2. **Scope.** Shadow mode only. Colorectal screening (positive FIT, and positive blood-based tests if agreed). Named sites: [site 1], [site 2], [site 3]. Duration: 90 days from [start date]. The Operator's system is read-only: it sends no message to any patient, writes nothing into the Provider's systems, assigns no task to anyone and gives no clinical advice. The Operator's system is not a diagnostic tool.

2a. **Compliance clock.** The pilot measures, per positive result, whether a referral to secondary care is requested within 15 working days of the result being released (DoH IFHAS V10, July 2025), and whether the colonoscopy follows within 31 working days of the first referral request. The parties will confirm before day 1 how each clock is counted: [from the release date / the date the Provider receives the result], and [to the referral request / to acceptance by the receiving unit].

3. **Data.** The Provider shares only the minimum fields: [list of fields]. A data-sharing agreement is signed before any real data is exchanged. Data stays in the UAE, in [the Provider's approved environment], with role-based access and an audit log. All data is deleted or returned within 30 days of the end, with a deletion certificate.

4. **Provider commitments.** A named sponsor ([name, role]); a coordinator for a weekly 30-minute review of flags; the export format and baseline volumes by [date]; staff-minute sampling for [number] weeks.

5. **Operator commitments.** Set-up and run; a weekly flag report; a readout at day 90 against the measures below; stop at once if any patient contact, write or privacy incident occurs.

6. **Success measures, agreed before start.**
   - S1 Export completeness: [at least 95%].
   - S2 Ownership visible with a running clock: [at least 95%].
   - S3 Untracked positives found: reported as a count and a rate; no target.
   - S4 Flag agreement with coordinators: [at least 80%].
   - S5 IFHAS 15-working-day referral baseline: reported.
   - S6 Blood-test 7-day and 30-day baseline: reported.
   - S7 Staff minutes by role: reported; no saving is promised.
   - S8 Patient messages, writes and privacy incidents: [zero]; any other value stops the run.

7. **Fees.** [At cost: AED [amount] / waived]. For reference, the Operator's modelled cost to serve for three clinics is about AED 106,000 (an estimate from assumed inputs, not a quote). Any active pilot is priced separately in a further written agreement.

7a. **Decision at day 90.** The Provider's sponsor decides against the measures in clause 6. The parties will consider an active pilot only if S1 and S2 are [at least 95%], S4 is [at least 80%], S8 is zero and the sponsor judges S3 worth acting on. Otherwise the pilot stops and data is deleted or returned as in clause 3.
