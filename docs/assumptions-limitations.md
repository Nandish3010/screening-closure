# Assumptions and limitations

What this prototype assumes, what it does not do, where each gap would bite, and how to remove it. All data is synthetic (see [data-provenance.md](data-provenance.md)). Items marked "to verify" were not established from a source we could read. Nothing here is a claim about real-world performance.

How to read the table:

- **Where it bites** uses five words: `simulator` (the behaviour model and its reported numbers), `engine` (the closure engine, API and scheduler), `data` (the synthetic cohort and results), `demo` (what a viewer sees), `deployment` (what a real pilot would need).
- **Source or rationale** points to the document, decision record or test where the item is explained. ADR numbers are in [project/DECISIONS.md](../project/DECISIONS.md).
- Review owners: H1 is the clinician review, H2 the native Arabic-speaker review (both pending, see [project/TRACKER.md](../project/TRACKER.md)).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L01 | All data is synthetic. Synthea needs a US state (Massachusetts), so names, addresses, phone numbers, facilities and care patterns are US. No Arabic names, no UAE demographics, languages or health-system structure. Language and contact channel come from simulator parameters, not from the cohort. | data, demo | ADR-005; [data-provenance.md](data-provenance.md) | The demo records look foreign. Nothing about the UAE population is validated. | Replace the cohort with a UAE-shaped synthetic generator, or a de-identified extract under a data agreement at pilot start. Add Arabic and English name variants to the test data. |
| L02 | The cohort currently loaded in HAPI was generated for ages 45-75. The code and the Synthea default now say 40-75 (A-fix), so a regenerated cohort will differ. | data, demo | [data-provenance.md](data-provenance.md); [evidence.md](evidence.md) section 8a | Ages 40-44 are absent from the demo cohort; any count shown in the demo does not match the code's eligibility rule. | Run `make demo-seed-clean && make demo-seed` before the feature freeze and refresh any quoted counts. |
| L03 | FIT results are generated by our own script: one result per patient, positive with probability `fit_positivity_rate`, drawn independently of the patient's clinical history. Negatives are not stored. No repeat tests, invitations or non-responders. | data, simulator | [data-provenance.md](data-provenance.md) | A positive patient is no more likely to have a colorectal condition in the record. The cohort shows pathway mechanics, not epidemiology. | Use real invitation and result feeds in a pilot (shadow mode, see [pilot-plan.md](pilot-plan.md)). |
| L04 | 87 of 131 simulator parameters are assumed, with no public source. Every reported absolute number is conditional on them. | simulator | `simulator/params.yaml`; table above | Reported shares and days (closure share, time to closure, open at 30, 60, 90 days) could be shifted. How far the gap between the two arms depends on them has not been tested across the assumed ranges. | Replace each assumed value with a pilot measurement. Run sensitivity sweeps with the command-line override (B10) over the ranges in [evidence.md](evidence.md). |
| L05 | The 16 calibrated parameters were tuned so the baseline arm reproduces the Mohl 2023 US completion curve. That the baseline matches the curve is by construction, not a validation. | simulator | [results/results.md](results/results.md), calibration section | The only Abu Dhabi follow-up figure found (2015-2016, FIT and gFOBT mixed, cut-off unknown) is lower than the calibration target. A different local baseline would change the size of any gain, in a direction we have not tested. | Calibrate on the pilot's own shadow-mode baseline. Run a scenario anchored on the Abu Dhabi figure. |
| L06 | The 15 sourced-adjacent values come from other settings: US safety-net clinics, a Hong Kong colonoscopy trial, English and Dutch programmes. Several evidence rows are marked S (read from a search summary, page not opened) and some sources contradict themselves. | simulator, data | [evidence.md](evidence.md) Gaps 8 and 9 | A value that does not transfer to Abu Dhabi, or a misread figure, would move results. To verify before citing. | Re-open every S row and check the primary source (E1 follow-up). Replace with local data when available. |
| L07 | Language shares (Arabic 0.45, English 0.35, other 0.20), preferred-channel shares and the baseline's single channel are assumed. Response differences by language are a calibrated multiplier, not a measurement. | simulator | `simulator/params.yaml` (`patients.*`, `patient_response.*`); ADR-005 | The fairness split by language and channel is an artefact of these inputs. It cannot show that the real programme is fair. | Measure language and channel mix and response in the pilot; get the Arabic review (H2) for the wording that drives response. |
| L08 | No capacity model. Staff and colonoscopy slots are unlimited, delays are independent random draws, and an escalation always finds someone to act with a fixed probability. The rejection reason "capacity" is a label, not a result of load. | simulator, deployment | `simulator/sim/engine.py`; [results/results.md](results/results.md) (escalations per 100 cases) | The orchestrated arm gets the benefit of escalation without a queueing cost. Coordinator workload and alert fatigue are not modelled. | Add slots per service per week and minutes per escalation from pilot data; until then treat escalations per 100 cases as a workload warning. |
| L09 | Effect size is at the upper range of the literature. The simulated orchestrated arm reaches 83.0% completion at 180 days against 52.1% at baseline, a ratio above the pooled navigation risk ratio of 1.27 (sourced). The orchestration effect probabilities (`orchestration.escalation_effect_prob`, `contact_recovery_prob`) are assumed. | simulator | [results/results.md](results/results.md) ("upper-range illustration"); `simulator/params.yaml` `navigation_effect` | Benefits are overstated if real escalations act less often or less effectively. Pilot targets set from the simulation could be unreachable. | Add a literature-anchored scenario that bounds the orchestration effect by the navigation risk ratio and report both (not yet run). Replace with measured pilot effects. |
| L10 | Confidence intervals are percentile bootstrap intervals over 20 simulation replications of 2,000 cases. They capture random-number noise only, not uncertainty in the parameters or in the model's structure. | simulator | [results/README.md](results/README.md) | Narrow intervals can be read as precision about the real world. They are not. | Add parameter sensitivity ranges to the evaluation (B10) and report them beside the intervals. |
| L11 | Working-day calendar: Monday to Friday are full working days, Saturday and Sunday the weekend, time zone Asia/Dubai, and the holiday list is empty. Moon-sighted holidays are not predictable and no source was chosen. Friday is a half day in government and some private employers and emirates differ; none of that is modelled. One calendar for all providers. | engine, simulator, deployment | ADR-009; `config/calendar.yaml`; [evidence.md](evidence.md) section 8d | Due dates come out earlier than reality around holidays, which is the conservative direction for a safety net but adds early overdue flags and noise. | Add holiday dates and per-provider calendars by configuration (no code change, tested in `api/tests/test_calendar.py`). Confirm the weekend convention against an official source per deployment. |
| L12 | SLA values: acknowledge 2, accept ownership 2, contact patient 5, patient acknowledges 7, book confirmatory 14, complete confirmatory 31, clinician review 5 working days. Only the 31 has a public anchor. The rest are design choices not reviewed by a clinician. There is no SLA on `close`, so a reviewed case awaiting sign-off never goes overdue in the engine. The simulator assumes a 3-working-day sign-off SLA (`orchestration.signoff_sla_working_days`) that the engine does not have. | engine, simulator | `config/pathways/colorectal_fit.yaml`; ADR-011; `api/app/pathway.py` (`sla_for_state`); `simulator/sim/engine.py` | Cases stuck at `clinician_reviewed` are not flagged by the sweep (only by the idle watchdog once built). SLAs may not match clinical practice. | Clinician review (H1). Add a `close` SLA to the YAML and have the simulator read it from there. |
| L13 | The 31-working-day target is the national follow-up colonoscopy measure counted from referral, as a programme-level percentage (above 90% acceptable). Our clock starts when `confirmatory_booked` is entered and applies to every case. The national guideline is itself inconsistent (15 working days in one clause, 31 in an appendix) and the IFHAS referral KPI is 15 working days. | engine, simulator | [evidence.md](evidence.md) section 8; ADR-013; pathway YAML | A case booked late can meet our SLA and still miss the national measure. KPI reporting would differ from the standard's definition. | Settle the clock start and the 15 versus 31 reading with the clinician reviewer (H1); report the share within target at programme level. |
| L14 | `mark_lost_contact` after 3 failed attempts is an assumption pending the clinician review. The engine checks only that the client-supplied `failed_attempts` is a number of at least 3. It does not count attempts, because no notifications log exists yet. | engine | ADR-011; `api/app/state_machine.py` | A case can be marked lost after fewer real attempts than 3; 3 may be too few or too many clinically. | Derive attempts from the notifications and call log (A7); set the number after H1. |
| L15 | FIT positivity of 0.07 assumes a sensitive cut-off (about 20 ug Hb/g class). Programmes with stricter cut-offs report 1.8% to 4.4%; the only Abu Dhabi figure is 13.5% (2015-2016, FIT and gFOBT mixed, cut-off not stated). | simulator, data, deployment | [evidence.md](evidence.md) section 1; `simulator/params.yaml` | Case volume, workload and cost per case scale with it. Completion among positives is not affected in the simulation. | Set it from the deploying programme's assay and cut-off; run sensitivity at 0.018 and 0.135. |
| L16 | The LOINC display string for 29771-3 changed between releases while the code did not. The display we ship was checked on one terminology server (LOINC 2.82). The code is authoritative. | Re-check at loinc.org and update the resource generator. |
| L17 | Arabic text is unreviewed (H2): message templates, the patient string catalogue, and the Arabic forbidden-phrase list.
