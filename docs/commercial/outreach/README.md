# Outreach kit: shadow pilot letter of intent and clinical review

Status: draft, 25 October 2026. Nothing here has been sent. No organisation or person has been contacted, and no relationship of any kind is claimed. The prototype runs on synthetic data only. It is not a diagnostic tool.

## What this kit is

Five short files to get two things: a signed, non-binding letter of intent for a read-only 90-day shadow pilot, and clinical mentors or reviewers.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| [contacts.md](contacts.md) | The kinds of contact to approach, what to ask each for, and a tracking table |
| [messages.md](messages.md) | Cold emails, LinkedIn notes, a mentor-booking message, an Arabic-reviewer request and a follow-up |
| [pilot-brief.md](pilot-brief.md) | One page to attach to every email. Export it as a PDF |
| [letter-of-intent.md](letter-of-intent.md) | Non-binding template, sent only when the other side shows interest |

Background: [go-to-market](../go-to-market.md) (the pilot proposal, KPIs S1 to S8 and exit criterion) and [buyer-map](../buyer-map.md) (who runs and pays for screening).

## Order of use

1. Pick a contact type in [contacts.md](contacts.md).
2. Send the matching cold email or LinkedIn note from [messages.md](messages.md).
3. Attach the [pilot brief](pilot-brief.md) as a PDF.
4. If they reply with interest, send the [letter of intent](letter-of-intent.md).
5. If there is no reply after 7 days, send the one follow-up. Do not send a second one.
6. Log every step in the tracking table in [contacts.md](contacts.md).

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] Every [bracket] is filled in. Search the text for "[" before sending.
- [ ] The pilot brief is attached as a PDF, not as markdown.
- [ ] No patient data is attached or pasted. The prototype uses synthetic data only.
- [ ] No relationship is claimed with any organisation. Say "I am approaching you", not "we work with".
- [ ] The message says "prototype on synthetic data" and does not say "results in patients".
- [ ] Plain language. No hype words.
- [ ] The letter of intent has been marked "Template. Not legal advice." and the provider's legal team will review it.

## What to say if asked

**Is it a medical device or a diagnostic tool?**
No. It does not diagnose anything and gives no clinical advice. It tracks who owns each positive result and how long it has been waiting against the official timelines. A clinician signs every closure.

**Does it contact patients?**
Not in shadow mode. The shadow run sends nothing to any patient, writes nothing into your systems and assigns no task to anyone.

**Where is the data hosted?**
In the provider's own approved environment in the UAE. No data leaves the country. A data-sharing agreement is signed before any real data is exchanged.

**What does it cost?**
The shadow run is offered at cost: about AED 106,000 to serve 3 clinics for 90 days. It can be scaled down to one clinic. Every cost input is an assumption, not a quote. See [go-to-market](../go-to-market.md) section 1.

**What do you need from us?**
A periodic export (HL7 v2 or CSV), a weekly 30-minute review by a coordinator, and a sponsor who signs the letter of intent.

**Has a clinician reviewed it?**
Not yet. That is part of the ask. A clinical review of the pathway and the patient wording is one of the things we are looking for.

If asked anything else (results, regulators, Malaffi, price at programme scale), say you will answer in writing and check [go-to-market](../go-to-market.md) and [buyer-map](../buyer-map.md) first. It is better to say "not known yet" than to guess.
