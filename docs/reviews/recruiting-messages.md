# Recruiting messages

Two short messages the owner can send. Fill in the square brackets before sending. Nothing here has been sent. Replace "[your name]" with your own name, and add a line about how you know the person if you do.

Attach to message (a): `clinician-review-pack.md` (print or save as PDF). Attach to message (b): `arabic-review-sheet.csv` (or the `.md` version, whichever opens more easily for them).

## (a) To find a clinician (doctor or nurse)

**Subject:** Two hours of clinical eyes on a health-screening prototype?

Hello [name],

I am working with a small team on a prototype for a health-screening hackathon (the Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge in Abu Dhabi). It tries to make sure that when a screening test comes back abnormal, the patient is not lost on the way to follow-up: someone is named as responsible, there are time limits, and a person is alerted when one is missed.

We have put some clinical numbers into it ourselves, such as how many days each step should take and when a blood pressure or blood sugar result counts as urgent. They are placeholders, and we would like a doctor or nurse to look at them and tell us what is wrong.

Would you be willing to give about two hours to go through a list of 35 items and mark each "agree", "change to ...", or "comment"? It is a reading task. There is no patient data at all, everything is synthetic, and you would not be asked to diagnose or advise anyone. "I cannot say" is a perfectly good answer.

If you agree, we will credit you in the submission in whatever way you prefer, by name or anonymously. I can send the list (about two pages, plus a table) so you can see what it involves before you decide. Even a "no, but try X" would help.

Thank you,
[your name]

## (b) To find an Arabic reviewer

**Subject:** Could you check some Arabic patient messages? About an hour and a half

Hello [name],

I am working with a small team on a prototype for a health-screening hackathon (the Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge in Abu Dhabi). It helps a care team follow up a patient after a screening test. The patient sees short messages and a simple page in Arabic and English.

The Arabic was written by us, with the help of AI, and no native speaker has checked it. I would be very grateful if you could read it and tell us where it sounds wrong, stiff, or confusing. The aim is plain, kind Arabic that a worried person in the Gulf would understand straight away.

It is about 190 short texts in a spreadsheet, each next to its English. For each one, write OK, or type the better wording, or leave a note. It should take about an hour and a half, and you can do it in pieces. There are no real patients or messages: everything is made-up sample data.

If you agree, we will credit you in the submission, by name or anonymously, as you prefer. I can send the sheet so you can see how it looks before you decide.

Thank you very much,
[your name]
