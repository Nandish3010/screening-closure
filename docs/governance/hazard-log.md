# Hazard log

Things that could go wrong for a patient, a clinician or the programme, how likely and how bad each is, what stops it, and the test that shows the control works. This is a design-time log for reviewers and the pilot partner. It is not a clinical safety case and not a compliance claim, and the ratings are the author's judgement, not measurements. The clinician review (H1) should revise them.

Related: the decision matrix ([decision-matrix.md](decision-matrix.md)) lists who may take each action; the limitations table ([../assumptions-limitations.md](../assumptions-limitations.md)) lists what is assumed or missing; the AI boundary ([ai-boundary.md](ai-boundary.md)) covers the model.

## How to read it

- **Likelihood** is how often the hazard would occur in a pilot if the controls listed were absent: L low, M medium, H high. **Impact** is the worst plausible consequence for a patient or the programme: L (inconvenience or a spoiled demo), M (delay or exposure of limited information), H (a patient left without follow-up, or health information disclosed).
- **Control** lists what stops or limits the hazard. `[built]` means it is in the repository today. `[pending X]` means ticket X builds it.
- **Test** cites the T-ID from [TEST-CASES.md](../../project/TEST-CASES.md) and the test name where one exists. "built" means the test is in the repository and passes. "Pending A11 (T-A32)" means the test does not exist yet; the ticket named will write it.
- **Residual risk** is the risk that remains once the controls listed are built, and states what is still open today where it differs. It is L, M or H with the reason.
- **Owner** is a role, not a person: Engine lead (Cut A), Simulator lead (Cut B), Interface lead (Cut C), AI-edge lead (Cut D), Governance lead (Cut E), Quality lead (Cut F), Demo lead (Cut E, tickets E8 and E9), Clinical lead (H1 reviewer), Programme coordinator (the pilot partner's role). Names are assigned at pilot start.
- Hazard ids are `HZ-nn`. They are not the same as the review tasks H1 and H2.

## Not covered here

- Harm from the clinical pathway itself (for example, whether a recommended colonoscopy is appropriate for a patient). That is the clinician's decision and is outside the system by design.
- Whether the product counts as regulated medical software in the UAE. Not established, to verify with the regulator.
- Security incident handling, backup and recovery, and staff training. These are deployment items for the operator (regulatory-mapping.md, ADHICS section).
- Hazards in a live Malaffi or messaging integration. They get their own entries when an interface is specified.

## Keeping it true

Each control should name a test. Completeness is checked by `make docs-check` (T-E7): every hazard has a likelihood, an impact and a control, and every control cites a test ID that exists in [TEST-CASES.md](../../project/TEST-CASES.md). When a pending ticket lands, replace "pending" with the test name in the same commit as the ticket. When a hazard is added, add its row here and its cause to the limitations table if the cause is a gap.
