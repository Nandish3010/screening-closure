# Contacts: the kinds of people and organisations to approach

These are types, not people. No individual is named, and no relationship is claimed with any organisation. Organisation names appear only where the [buyer map](../buyer-map.md) already names them. Message letters refer to [messages.md](messages.md). Asks: LOI = signed letter of intent ([template](letter-of-intent.md)); H1 = clinical review of the pathway; H2 = native Arabic review of patient wording.

| Contact type | Where to find them | Ask | Message | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hackathon mentors (clinical or health-system) | The challenge's mentor sessions | Mentorship, H1, introductions | (d) | High |
| Health teams of the challenge's co-creator organisations | See the challenge page | Introduction to a programme or quality lead; LOI | (a) | High |
| Quality or population-health departments of IFHAS provider networks (for example the public clinic networks and private groups in the buyer map) | Organisation websites and official directories; ask via mentors first | LOI for a shadow run | (a) | High |
| Screening programme leads at the public health centre that runs IFHAS | Public programme pages; introductions | Sponsor, introduction, later a programme pilot | (a) | Medium |
| Gastroenterology departments | Hospital and clinic department pages; mentors | H1 clinical review, introduction | (b) | High |
| Primary-care departments | Clinic network pages; mentors | H1 clinical review, introduction | (b) | High |
| Hub71 / DoH health-tech accelerator programme staff | Programme pages and published contact routes | Introduction to a sponsor; regulatory dialogue | (a) or (d) | Medium |
| University public-health faculty | Faculty department pages | Mentorship, H1 review, introduction | (b) | Medium |
| Native Arabic-speaking health communicators | Professional networks; university health-communication programmes; mentors | H2 Arabic review of patient wording | (f) | Medium |
